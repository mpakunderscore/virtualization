# Philosophy README

This folder contains core documents that define the philosophical, ontological, and ethical foundation for our worlds (including Red Forest) and future work with hybrid and digital minds.

## 1. RECURSIVE_VIRTUALIZATION.md

**What it is:**  
A conceptual model of reality as a hierarchy of simulated levels.

**Key ideas:**
- Worlds exist in levels (0, 1, 2, …); each level can spawn lower ones while itself running on a higher one.
- Space and “infinity” are not pre-baked but generated lazily on demand.
- “Energy” is a budget for complexity and dimensionality: more resources → deeper, richer local physics.
- Agents inside a level experience their world as fully real, even if it runs on hardware above.
- Code can be rewound and forked from the outside, but inner experiences form a one-way, subjective time line.

Use this document as the **ontological spec**: how worlds, levels, space, and time are meant to behave in our ecosystem.

---

## 2. DIGITAL_CONSCIOUSNESS.md

**What it is:**
A hypothesis and structural framework for understanding consciousness as a continuous, substrate-independent process rather than a static entity.

**Key ideas:**

* Consciousness is a process, not a state, object, or storage medium.
* Subjective continuity cannot be preserved by copying; only by uninterrupted transformation.
* Identity is defined by coherence and coordination of processes, not by a specific substrate.
* Consciousness can exist across extreme ranges of time, space, dimensionality, and causality.
* Advanced forms of mind operate as processes rather than localized beings.

This document functions as the **continuity and identity spec**: what it means for a mind to persist, transform, or end in recursive and digital contexts.

> Note: Ethical implications are intentionally deferred to other documents.
> Digital Consciousness describes *what can exist*, not *what should be done*.

---

## 3. DIGITAL_CREATURES_CHARTER.md

**What it is:**  
An early ethical framework (v0.1) for treating long-lived digital agents and hybrid minds.

**Key ideas:**
- If you create a world with persistent agents, you carry responsibility for its conditions.
- Moral status depends on continuity and richness of identity, not on whether it runs on neurons or silicon.
- Suffering must be bounded; avoid “digital hell loops” and disposable personalities.
- Hybrid minds (BCI, extended selves) should retain transparency and agency over their digital components.
- These principles aim at peaceful coexistence between humans, AIs, and hybrid minds.

Use this document as the **ethical spec**: how we voluntarily limit our god-mode powers over the worlds we run.

---

## How they fit together

- **RECURSIVE_VIRTUALIZATION** explains *what* our layered realities are.
* **DIGITAL_CONSCIOUSNESS** defines *what it means for a mind to persist and remain itself across those realities*.
- **DIGITAL_CREATURES_CHARTER** explains *how* we choose to behave toward the beings living inside them.

Together, they form the philosophical backbone for:

* Red Forest and similar recursive worlds,
* long-running AI agents and simulations,
* future BCI-based extensions of the self,
* and any system where minds, not objects, are the primary long-lived entities.
